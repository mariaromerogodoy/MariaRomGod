Make Me Perfect

Finding beauty in mistakes and imperfect images and the things we usually try to fix

Book DesignExperimental DesignCreative CodingWeb Experience

The idea

As a designer, I am always looking for inspiration, even in things that don't seem beautiful at first. Make Me Perfect explores how mistakes, unusual images, and things that feel out of place can become beautiful when you look at them differently.

I created a print book and a digital experience to question why design is always expected to be perfect.

Process

I collected images that felt imperfect and experimented with placing them at slanted angles to create a kind of structured imperfection. The book uses red throughout because it is a color we often associate with mistakes and things going wrong.

I chose Helvetica because it is often seen as such a perfect typeface. I thought it would be funny to start with something so clean and controlled, then distort it and make it imperfect on purpose.

The contrast between the organized type and red layouts became the visual language of the book.

From print to digital

I expanded the idea into an interactive website built with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. Rather than trying to make every interaction perfectly polished, the site embraces rough edges, play, and experimentation.
