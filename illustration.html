WIRED Article Illustration

A coded illustration exploring how our differences can also bring us together

Creative CodingIllustrationWeb Design

This is an Independent concept project / a fictional WIRED article presentation created as a design mockup, not commissioned by or affiliated with WIRED

01 / The idea

What makes us different

This project started with a speculative illustration mockup inspired by the WIRED article “Human Design Is Blowing Up. Following It Might Make You Leave Your Spouse.” I wanted to explore how people can be completely different from one another, but still find ways to connect

I created a digital illustration using JavaScript, HTML, and CSS, bringing the idea of Human Design into a visual and interactive experience

02 / Visual system

Five types, five colors

Each of the five colors represents one of the five Human Design types. Some parts of the figures connect and overlap to suggest compatibility, while others stay separate to show individuality

I liked the idea of creating people out of little pieces that don't all have to fit together perfectly

The coded illustration in the editorial context

03 / From code to interaction

An illustration that moves

Instead of making a static image, I explored what happens when illustration becomes a digital experience. Through code and motion, the figures feel like they are constantly changing, connecting, and separating

The project brings together illustration and front-end development, while experimenting with how an editorial image can become interactive
